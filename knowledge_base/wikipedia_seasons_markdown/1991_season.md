# 1991 Formula One World Championship

**Source**: Wikipedia (2026-01-14T21:45:51.290484)

The1991 FIA Formula One World Championshipwas the 45th season ofFIAFormula Onemotor racing and the 42nd season of the Formula One World Championship. It featured the 1991 Formula One World Championship for Drivers and the 1991 Formula One World Championship for Constructors, which were contested concurrently over a sixteen-race series that commenced on 10 March and ended on 3 November.
